## Quarter 2 Project Brainstorming

**1. What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

One of the most interesting topics we've explored this quarter is the use of large language models and retrieval systems to improve technical support workflows. Through our collaboration with Bosch, I've learned about the challenges of interpreting technical queries, identifying equipment and part numbers, and retrieving relevant information from specialized documentation. I find the intersection of natural language processing, information retrieval, and practical industry applications particularly interesting, especially when the system needs to provide accurate and reliable responses rather than simply generate plausible answers.

**2. Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.**

For Quarter 2, I would be interested in investigating how different retrieval and query-understanding approaches affect the accuracy and efficiency of an AI-powered HVAC technical support assistant. Specifically, I would like to compare traditional keyword matching, fuzzy search, and embedding-based semantic retrieval, potentially combined with an LLM for query classification and response generation. The goal would be to identify which combination performs best across different types of user requests, including equipment identification, troubleshooting questions, and queries containing incomplete or incorrect information.

**3. What is a potential change you'd make to the approach taken in your current Quarter 1 Project?**

One change I would consider is placing greater emphasis on developing a systematic evaluation framework earlier in the project. Rather than primarily focusing on building the search and answer workflow, I would create a representative collection of test queries with expected results and use it to measure retrieval accuracy, response quality, and latency. This would make it easier to compare alternative approaches, identify failure cases, and determine whether additional complexity, such as LLM-based classification or semantic search, actually improves performance over simpler methods.

**4. What other techniques would you be interested in using in your project?**

I would be interested in exploring retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), vector embeddings, hybrid search, and reranking techniques. Hybrid search could combine exact keyword matching for equipment identifiers with semantic similarity for more descriptive troubleshooting questions. I would also like to experiment with query rewriting, confidence scoring, and structured validation to reduce hallucinations and improve reliability. These techniques could help make the assistant more robust when dealing with ambiguous user requests, technical terminology, or incomplete information.
